Our coffee Products.

At Aroma Coffee and Tea House, we offer premium Kenyan coffee sourced directly from local farmers.All our coffee is 100% organic and freshly roasted for the best aroma and taste.

  • Arabica coffee: Known for its smooth flavor and aromatic qualities, our Arabica coffee is a favorite among coffee enthusiasts.
  • Robusta coffee: With a stronger and more robust flavor, our Robusta coffee is perfect for those who enjoy a bold cup of coffee.
  • Kenyan Peaberry coffee: This unique coffee variety is known for its distinct flavor profile and is a must-try for coffee connoisseurs.

Our Tea Products.

We also offer a variety of high-quality teas, including black, white and herbal blends. Our teas are carefully selected to provide a delightful experience for tea lovers.

  • Black tea: Our black tea is rich and full-bodied, perfect for a morning boost or an afternoon pick-me-up.
  • Green tea: Known for its health benefits and refreshing taste, our green tea is a favorite among health-conscious individuals.
  • Herbal infusions: Our herbal teas are made from a variety of herbs and botanicals, offering a soothing and calming experience.
